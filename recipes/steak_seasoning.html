Steak Seasoning

Photo by Yazan Abedelkhaleq on Unsplash

Description

The following recipe is a salt-based seasoning for steak or other beef cuts, such as tri-tip or prime rib.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp sea-salt
  • 1 tbsp garlic salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp rosemary

Steps

For mixing the ingredients, I recommend using an empty shaker bottle for easy storage. Otherwise, you can use a standard mixing bowl.

  1. Mix seas-salt, garlic salt, black pepper. Italian seasoning, and rosemary into mixing device of your choice.
  2. Double or triple the ingredients if you want more seasoning for larger beef cuts.
