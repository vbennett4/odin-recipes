Greek Pasta Salad

Photo by engin akyurt on Unsplash

Description

The following recipe is for a traditional Greek pasta salad that yields four servings. Pepperoni sausage is used for flavor, but it is optional if a vegetarian dish is preferred.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp basil
  • 1 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper
  • 3/4 tsp sugar
  • 2 1/2 cup cooked pasta
  • 3 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 15 cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup sliced red bell peppers
  • 3/4 cup feta cheese
  • 1/2 cup chopped green onions
  • 4 oz black olives
  • 3/4 cup sliced pepperoni sausage (Optional)

Steps

The following steps assume you are using a large mixing bowl:
  1. Whisk 1/2 cup olive oil, 1/2 cup red wine vinegar, 1 1/2 tsp garlic poweder, 1 1/2 tsp basil, 1 1/2 tsp oregano, 3/4 tsp black pepper, and 3/4 tsp sugar in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Add 2 1/2 cup cooked pasta, 3 cup sliced mushrooms, 15 halved cherry tomatoes, 1 cup sliced red bell peppers, 3/4 cup feta cheese, 1/2 cup chopped green onions, 4 oz black olives, and 3/4 cup sliced pepperoni sausage into the bowl.
  3. Mixed all ingredients until evenly coated.
  4. Cover the mixing bowl and refrigerate it for about 2 hours.
