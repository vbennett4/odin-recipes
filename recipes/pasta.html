Tomato Sauce

Photo by Ben Lei on Unsplash

Description

The following recipe is for a tomato sauce that's traditionally used for pasta, but it can be used for any dish you wish. The recipe can make about 8 average-sized servings of pasta.

The sauce is on the saltier side of taste, but can be sweetened with more sugar if preferred. Hot Italian sausage is used for flavor, but it isn't necessary if a vegetarian dish is preferred as well.
One final note, instructions for this tomato sauce recipe will be specifically using a crockpot for cooking. If a different cooking apparatus is used, such as a stove pot, cooking time may vary.

Ingredients

The following is a list of ingredients used in this recipe, some of which are optional:

  • 28 oz. crushed tomatoes
  • 12 oz. tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 tsp garlic salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp basil
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 19 oz. Italian sausage (optional)

Steps

The following steps will be assuming you're using a crockpot to cook the sauce. If you are not, cooking times may vary.

  1. Mix in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, garlic salt, oregano, basil, salt, sugar, and black pepper into the crockpot. Keep mixing until all ingredients appear to be evenly mixed.
  2. Optional: Insert Italian sausage, making sure it's submerged under the sauce.
  3. Set the crockpot to cook on High for 2-4 hours
